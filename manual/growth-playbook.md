
# TikTok Growth Playbook

## 1. Purpose

This playbook turns the TikTok Growth Intelligence System into a practical growth operation for:

1. Travel Alerts.ke
2. Simo Traveller

The objective is to build two credible brands with distinct audiences through research, valuable content, consistent production and evidence-led improvement.

Growth should be measured by audience quality and meaningful engagement, not views alone.

## 2. Growth Principles

Every growth decision should follow these principles:

- Audience value comes before posting volume.
- Strong content begins with a clear audience need.
- Research should inform content decisions.
- Each brand must maintain a distinct identity.
- Retention and engagement matter alongside reach.
- Performance should be measured consistently.
- Experiments should test specific hypotheses.
- No single video guarantees future performance.
- Trust is a long-term growth asset.

## 3. Brand-Specific Growth Strategies

### A. Travel Alerts.ke

**Positioning:** A useful travel intelligence and destination information source focused primarily on Africa.

Primary audience:
- African travellers.
- Kenyan travellers.
- People planning international trips.
- Travel professionals.
- People seeking current travel developments.

Growth opportunities:

1. Become a reliable source of timely travel developments.
2. Explain how policy and travel changes affect real travellers.
3. Build recurring coverage of African destinations.
4. Make complex visa, border and aviation developments easy to understand.
5. Develop useful destination, hotel and flight opportunity content.
6. Encourage viewers to save and share practical information.

Content priorities:
- Breaking travel news.
- Destination alerts.
- Visa, passport and immigration changes.
- Airport, airline and route developments.
- Flight discounts and travel opportunities.
- Destination and hospitality insights.

A travel alert should answer:
- What happened?
- Who is affected?
- Why does it matter?
- What should travellers do?
- Where can viewers verify the information?

Do not sacrifice accuracy for speed.

### B. Simo Traveller

**Positioning:** An African travel discovery and practical travel storytelling brand.

Primary audience:
- Budget-conscious travellers.
- African travel enthusiasts.
- Kenyan domestic travellers.
- People interested in nature, wildlife and outdoor experiences.
- Viewers planning African trips.

Growth opportunities:

1. Build recognisable destination series.
2. Make lesser-known places easier to discover.
3. Produce useful travel guides.
4. Combine scenery with informative voiceovers.
5. Develop a recognisable storytelling style.
6. Build trust through useful, well-researched information.
7. Convert interested viewers into returning followers and YouTube subscribers.

Content priorities:
- African destinations.
- Hidden gems.
- Wildlife and safari.
- Hiking and outdoor travel.
- Budget travel guides.
- Transport and accommodation.
- Travel stories and practical advice.

Do not imply first-hand experience where none exists.

## 4. Content Discovery and Research

Use the research resources:

- `Agent/research-methodology.md`
- `research/trending-topics.md`
- `research/competitor-analysis.md`

For each proposed topic, evaluate:

1. Audience relevance.
2. Timeliness.
3. Search interest or discovery potential.
4. Distinctiveness.
5. Evidence quality.
6. Production feasibility.
7. Potential audience value.

A topic should not be selected simply because it is trending.

For Travel Alerts.ke, freshness and verification are essential.

For Simo Traveller, practical usefulness and destination appeal are essential.

## 5. Content Planning

Plan content separately for each brand.

A practical weekly planning process:

### Step 1: Discover
Identify current developments, audience questions and content opportunities.

### Step 2: Evaluate
Score topics for relevance, evidence, audience value and distinctiveness.

### Step 3: Select
Choose a manageable number of strong topics.

### Step 4: Schedule
Assign each topic a production and publication date.

### Step 5: Review
Check the balance of content pillars and avoid unnecessary repetition.

Do not force a weak topic into the schedule just to maintain posting frequency.

## 6. Video Growth Framework

Use this structure as a starting point:

### Hook
Give viewers a clear reason to continue watching.

### Value
Deliver the most important information early.

### Development
Explain the context and supporting details.

### Practical takeaway
Give viewers something useful to remember, do or explore.

### Closing
Use a relevant call to action.

The opening should accurately represent what the video delivers.

Avoid misleading curiosity gaps and exaggerated promises.

## 7. TikTok Search and Discovery

Use `Algorithm/tiktok-seo.md` and `Algorithm/Recommendation-signals.md`.

For every video:

- Identify the main search topic.
- Understand the audience's likely search intent.
- Use relevant keywords naturally in the script.
- Include clear keywords in captions where appropriate.
- Use descriptive on-screen text.
- Select relevant hashtags.
- Keep the video focused on one main subject.

Review TikTok search and discovery opportunities regularly.

Do not assume hashtags alone will generate reach.

## 8. Retention and Engagement

Study how viewers respond to each video.

Important measurements include:

- Average watch time.
- Video completion rate.
- Rewatches where available.
- Shares.
- Saves.
- Comments.
- Profile visits.
- Followers gained.

Interpret metrics in context.

For example:
- Low watch time may indicate a weak opening, slow pacing or a mismatch between promise and delivery.
- Strong saves may indicate practical or reference value.
- Strong shares may indicate relevance to a wider audience.
- Views without follower growth may indicate weak brand connection or unclear reasons to follow.

These are diagnostic possibilities, not automatic conclusions. Examine the content and comparable videos before deciding.

## 9. Publishing and Consistency

Build a sustainable publishing routine.

Prioritise:
- Quality and relevance.
- Consistent production.
- Appropriate timing.
- Audience interaction.
- Timely responses to important developments.

Do not copy another creator's schedule without testing whether it works for the brand.

Publishing remains subject to human approval unless explicit authority has been granted.

## 10. Analytics and Weekly Review

Use:
- `analytics/performance-tracker.csv`
- `analytics/weekly-reports.md`

Review each brand independently.

Every week, answer:

1. Which videos performed best?
2. Which videos underperformed?
3. What topics attracted the right audience?
4. Which hooks and formats deserve further testing?
5. What drove saves and shares?
6. Did videos generate profile visits and followers?
7. What should be repeated, improved or stopped?

Use comparable measurement periods.

Avoid drawing strong conclusions from very small samples.

## 11. Controlled Experimentation

Use `experiments/testing-log.md`.

Test one main variable at a time, such as:

- Opening hook.
- Video duration.
- Voiceover structure.
- Visual format.
- Caption approach.
- Posting time.
- Call to action.

For each experiment:

1. State the hypothesis.
2. Record the current approach.
3. Define the test approach.
4. Select the success metric.
5. Record the results.
6. Evaluate the evidence.
7. Decide what to do next.

Do not change the entire content strategy because of one unusual result.

## 12. Competitor Intelligence

Use `research/competitor-analysis.md`.

Study relevant creators and publishers to understand:

- Topics they cover.
- Their content formats.
- Opening hooks.
- Audience response.
- Content gaps.
- Recurring series.
- Opportunities to provide better information.

Learn from patterns, not imitation.

Do not copy scripts, branding or original creative work.

## 13. Growth Milestones

Use milestones to evaluate progress rather than relying on arbitrary viral targets.

### Foundation
- Clear brand positioning.
- Consistent content quality.
- Reliable research and production.
- Accurate performance records.

### Early traction
- Repeatable content formats emerge.
- Audience engagement becomes more consistent.
- Returning viewers and followers increase.
- Stronger understanding of audience interests.

### Developing growth
- Recognisable content series.
- Improved search discovery.
- More consistent follower conversion.
- Evidence-backed production decisions.

### Sustainable growth
- Established audience trust.
- Repeatable production systems.
- Strong brand recognition.
- Relevant partnerships and monetisation opportunities.

Set numerical targets only after reviewing each account's baseline performance.

## 14. Monetisation and Long-Term Development

### Travel Alerts.ke

Potential opportunities:
- Relevant travel partnerships.
- Hotel and accommodation promotions.
- Airline and travel-service campaigns.
- Clearly disclosed sponsored content.
- Travel-related affiliate opportunities.

Editorial independence must be protected.

### Simo Traveller

Potential opportunities:
- YouTube monetisation.
- Hotel and lodge partnerships.
- Destination and tourism collaborations.
- Travel-related affiliate links.
- Sponsored travel content.
- Future travel services and products.

Never present paid promotion as independent editorial judgement.

Verify commercial terms and disclose partnerships appropriately.

## 15. Monthly Strategy Review

At the end of each month:

1. Review performance for both brands.
2. Identify the strongest content pillars.
3. Compare formats and retention.
4. Review follower and audience growth.
5. Assess search and discovery performance.
6. Review completed experiments.
7. Identify audience questions and content gaps.
8. Set the next month's priorities.

Update the strategy only when evidence or meaningful changes justify it.

## 17. Final Growth Principle

The goal is not to chase every trend or manufacture viral content.

The goal is to understand audiences, publish useful material, measure real outcomes and improve decisions continuously.

Build trust first. Use evidence to guide growth. Keep both brands distinctive.
