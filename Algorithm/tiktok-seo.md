
# TikTok SEO and Search Discovery

## Purpose

This document explains how to make videos easier to discover through TikTok Search.

It provides practical search optimisation guidance for:
- Travel Alerts.ke
- Simo Traveller

The objective is to reach people actively searching for useful travel information, not simply to chase viral trends.

TikTok's search system considers how well content matches a search query. TikTok identifies content information such as hashtags and sounds, alongside user interactions and user information, as factors in search discovery.

Do not claim to know TikTok's private ranking formula.

## 1. Core Search Optimisation Principles

### A. Identify the search intent

Before producing a video, identify the question or information need behind the search.

Examples:
- What documents do I need to travel to Tanzania?
- Is the Kenya-Uganda border open?
- How much does a Maasai Mara safari cost?
- Best hiking destinations in Kenya.
- Are there direct flights from Nairobi to Kigali?

Create content that answers a real question.

### B. Use one primary keyword

Each video should have one clear primary search phrase.

Examples:
- Kenya travel requirements
- Tanzania visa requirements
- Nairobi to Kigali flights
- Mount Kenya hiking guide
- Zimbabwe travel advisory

Use related phrases naturally, but do not make the video target unrelated topics.

### C. Keep the topic consistent

Where appropriate, communicate the main topic through:
1. The opening spoken words or voiceover.
2. On-screen headline text.
3. The caption.
4. Relevant hashtags.

This is a practical consistency strategy, not a claim that every element has an equal ranking weight.

### D. Answer the search query

Do not use a keyword simply to attract views.

The video must deliver the information promised by its headline.

Avoid misleading headlines, unrelated trending terms and keyword stuffing.

## 2. Keyword Research Workflow

Use these methods before selecting a video topic.

### Method 1: TikTok search suggestions

1. Open TikTok Search.
2. Enter a broad topic.
3. Observe suggested search phrases.
4. Test specific questions and destination names.
5. Review the videos appearing for those searches.
6. Record useful content gaps and unanswered questions.

Examples:
- Kenya travel
- Kenya travel requirements
- Kenya travel costs
- Kenya safari destinations
- Africa border updates

Search suggestions are research clues, not proof of search volume.

### Method 2: Creator Search Insights

TikTok introduced Creator Search Insights to help creators discover topics people search for.

Access:
1. Open TikTok.
2. Search for "Creator Search Insights".
3. Open the feature if it is available to your account.
4. Explore suggested topics and relevant categories.
5. Check the Content gap filter.
6. Record promising topics before producing videos.

Content gaps are topics with search demand but comparatively fewer videos.

Availability and displayed features may vary by account and region. Do not assume the tool is available to every Kenyan account.

### Method 3: Audience questions

Use:
- Comments on your own videos.
- Questions under relevant travel videos.
- Questions from travellers and tourism communities.
- Repeated questions about borders, visas, flights and destinations.

Turn recurring questions into searchable video topics.

## 3. Travel Alerts.ke Search Strategy

### Brand positioning

Travel Alerts.ke provides practical travel intelligence, with an emphasis on Africa.

Content should help travellers understand:
- What has changed?
- Which travellers are affected?
- When does the change take effect?
- What should travellers do?

### Main keyword categories

**Border and immigration**
- Africa border updates
- Kenya border crossing
- Uganda entry requirements
- Tanzania immigration updates
- African passport requirements

**Visas and entry requirements**
- Kenya visa requirements
- Tanzania visa for Kenyans
- Rwanda entry requirements
- African eVisa updates
- Visa-free African countries

**Airlines and airports**
- Nairobi airport updates
- Africa flight disruptions
- New African flight routes
- Airline travel updates
- Flight cancellation advice

**Travel advisories**
- Africa travel advisory
- Zimbabwe travel update
- Kenya security travel advice
- Middle East travel advisory
- China travel requirements

**Tourism and travel industry**
- Africa tourism news
- African hotel industry
- Sustainable tourism Africa
- New destinations in Africa
- African travel opportunities

### Recommended video structure

1. Opening: state the development and location.
2. Context: explain what happened.
3. Impact: identify affected travellers.
4. Action: explain what travellers should do.
5. Source: identify the responsible authority or official notice.

### Example

Primary keyword: Tanzania entry requirements for Kenyans

Opening:
"Planning to travel from Kenya to Tanzania? Here are the entry requirements you should check before departure."

On-screen headline:
"Tanzania Entry Requirements for Kenyans"

Caption:
"Travelling from Kenya to Tanzania? Check the latest official entry requirements, required travel documents and any applicable health or immigration rules before departure. Always confirm with the relevant authorities."

Hashtags:
#TanzaniaTravel #KenyaTravel #TravelRequirements #TravelAlertsKe #EastAfrica

Important: Verify entry requirements against current official sources before publishing. Never present old information as a current alert.

## 4. Simo Traveller Search Strategy

### Brand positioning

Simo Traveller helps people discover and explore Kenya and Africa through practical travel guides, destination experiences, scenery and useful travel information.

The content should answer questions that help viewers plan real trips.

### Main keyword categories

**Destinations**
- Best places to visit in Kenya
- Places to visit in Tanzania
- Best destinations in Zambia
- Zimbabwe tourism
- Botswana travel guide

**Wildlife and safaris**
- Maasai Mara safari guide
- Amboseli National Park
- African wildlife safari
- Kenya safari costs
- Best time to visit Maasai Mara

**Hiking and outdoor travel**
- Hiking in Kenya
- Mount Kipipiri hiking
- Aberdare Forest hiking
- Mount Suswa hiking
- Camping in Kenya

**Travel planning**
- Kenya travel budget
- African travel costs
- How to travel by SGR in Kenya
- Best mid-range hotels in Kenya
- Solo travel in Africa

**Transport and accommodation**
- Kenya SGR travel guide
- Nairobi to Mombasa train
- Hotels in Watamu
- Accommodation in Zambia
- Getting around Zimbabwe

### Recommended video structure

1. Hook: introduce the destination or question.
2. Visual introduction: show the destination.
3. Practical information: costs, transport, access and requirements.
4. Experience: explain what makes the place worth visiting.
5. Next step: invite viewers to save, share or follow for more guides.

### Example

Primary keyword: Mount Suswa hiking guide

Opening:
"Looking for a hiking destination in Kenya beyond the usual trails? Let's explore Mount Suswa."

On-screen headline:
"Mount Suswa Hiking Guide | Kenya"

Caption:
"Planning a Mount Suswa hike? Here is what to know about the route, preparation, transport and what to expect. Confirm current access arrangements and local guide requirements before travelling."

Hashtags:
#MountSuswa #HikingKenya #KenyaTravel #OutdoorAdventure #SimoTraveller

Never invent first-hand experiences, current prices or access conditions.

## 5. Caption Writing Framework

Use this simple structure:

1. Primary keyword in a natural opening sentence.
2. A short explanation of what the viewer will learn.
3. Important practical details.
4. A relevant call to action.
5. A small group of accurate hashtags.

Captions should be written for people first.

Do not repeat the same keyword unnaturally or use unrelated popular hashtags.

## 6. Search-Friendly Video Production Checklist

Before publishing, check:

- [ ] One clearly defined search topic.
- [ ] A useful opening that identifies the subject.
- [ ] Clear and readable headline text.
- [ ] Accurate information that answers the query.
- [ ] Relevant spoken explanation or voiceover.
- [ ] Descriptive caption.
- [ ] Relevant hashtags.
- [ ] Correct location and destination names.
- [ ] Reliable sources for factual travel information.
- [ ] No misleading claims or invented details.

## 7. Performance Measurement

Where TikTok analytics provide the information, monitor:

- Search traffic or search-driven views.
- Search queries bringing viewers to the video.
- Average watch time.
- Video completion rate.
- Saves and shares.
- Comments and questions.
- Profile visits.
- New followers.

Do not judge SEO success from total views alone.

A video with fewer views but strong search traffic, saves and relevant followers may be valuable.

Review performance after a consistent testing period. Compare similar topics and formats rather than drawing conclusions from one video.

## 8. Testing Framework

For each experiment, record:

- Brand.
- Video topic.
- Primary keyword.
- Search intent.
- Opening hook.
- Caption.
- Publication date.
- Search traffic, if available.
- Watch time and completion.
- Saves and shares.
- New followers.
- Lessons learned.

Change one major variable at a time where practical.

Do not assume a successful result proves a universal TikTok ranking rule.

## 9. Important SEO Mistakes to Avoid

- Using popular but unrelated hashtags.
- Making promises the video does not fulfil.
- Using vague headlines.
- Targeting too many unrelated keywords in one video.
- Publishing outdated border or visa information.
- Copying competitors without adding value.
- Repeating identical captions across different videos.
- Assuming a high view count means strong search performance.
- Treating search suggestions as verified search-volume data.
- Assuming that every keyword must appear a fixed number of times.
