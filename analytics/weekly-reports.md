
# TikTok Growth Intelligence — Weekly Performance Reports

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how the AI agent reviews weekly content performance and recommends improvements for:

- Travel Alerts.ke
- Simo Traveller

The objective is to turn performance data into better content decisions, stronger audience relationships and sustainable growth.

Do not judge performance using views alone.

## 2. Reporting Principles

The agent must:

- Analyse each brand separately.
- Use actual recorded performance data.
- Identify missing or incomplete measurements.
- Compare similar videos and formats where possible.
- Distinguish facts from explanations and hypotheses.
- Identify both successful and unsuccessful content.
- Avoid declaring a winner from one video.
- Recommend specific actions for the following week.
- Never invent missing analytics.
- Account for differences in video age and measurement periods.

## 3. Reporting Period

Use a consistent weekly reporting period.

Default:
- Monday to Sunday.
- Prepare the report after the reporting period closes.
- Record the reporting dates.
- Include the date the report was generated.

Where possible, compare:
- Current week.
- Previous week.
- Four-week trend.

Do not compare incomplete weeks as if they were complete.

## 4. Required Data

Use the performance tracker to collect:

- Videos published.
- Views.
- Reach, where available.
- Likes.
- Comments.
- Shares.
- Saves.
- Average watch time.
- Full-video completion rate.
- Profile views.
- Followers gained.
- Traffic sources.
- Video duration.
- Content pillar.
- Video format.
- Publication date.

Identify missing metrics before interpreting results.

## 5. Key Performance Indicators

### Publishing activity
- Number of videos published.
- Publishing consistency.
- Distribution across content pillars.

### Reach and discovery
- Total views.
- Average views per video.
- Median views per video.
- Reach, where available.

Median views help reduce the influence of one unusually successful video.

### Engagement
- Likes.
- Comments.
- Shares.
- Saves.

Where the required denominator is available, calculate:

Engagement rate by views:

(Likes + Comments + Shares) / Views × 100

Save rate by views:

Saves / Views × 100

Share rate by views:

Shares / Views × 100

Always state which formula was used.

### Audience retention
- Average watch time.
- Video duration.
- Average watch time as a proportion of duration.
- Full-video completion rate, where available.

Do not assume average watch time and completion rate are interchangeable.

### Audience growth
- Followers gained.
- Profile views.
- Follower conversion from profile views, where data permits.

Follower conversion:

Followers gained / Profile views × 100

Do not calculate a rate when the denominator is zero or unavailable.

## 6. Brand-Specific Review

### A. Travel Alerts.ke

Assess:

- Which travel developments attracted attention?
- Which alerts generated shares and saves?
- Which topics produced meaningful questions?
- Did practical traveller instructions improve engagement?
- Which African regions received coverage?
- Was coverage geographically balanced?
- Were videos timely and sufficiently sourced?
- Did viewers visit the profile or follow after viewing?

Prioritise accuracy, relevance, timeliness and audience trust.

High views do not justify weak or sensational reporting.

### B. Simo Traveller

Assess:

- Which destinations attracted interest?
- Which travel experiences generated engagement?
- Which videos encouraged people to save or share?
- Did voiceover and scenery hold attention?
- Which practical guides answered audience needs?
- Which topics attracted new followers?
- Which content encouraged meaningful discussion?
- Which recurring series deserve continuation?

Prioritise audience loyalty, useful travel information and distinctive storytelling.

## 7. Content Format Analysis

Compare performance by:

- Video duration.
- Hook style.
- Voiceover versus text-led presentation.
- Destination footage versus explanatory visuals.
- Breaking news versus evergreen information.
- Practical guide versus storytelling.
- Single-topic video versus roundup.

Only compare formats where sufficient data exists.

Do not conclude that a format works better based on one video.

## 8. Weekly Review Questions

Answer the following:

1. What performed best this week?
2. What performed weakest?
3. Which topics generated the strongest audience response?
4. Which hooks appear promising?
5. Which formats deserve further testing?
6. Did the audience ask recurring questions?
7. Did any content attract followers?
8. What should be repeated?
9. What should be improved?
10. What should be stopped?

Support conclusions with recorded data.

## 9. Underperforming Content Diagnosis

When a video performs below expectations, investigate:

- Topic relevance.
- Hook clarity.
- Audience targeting.
- Opening seconds.
- Video length.
- Information density.
- Visual quality.
- Story structure.
- Search relevance.
- Publishing context.
- Data collection timing.

Treat explanations as hypotheses unless the available evidence supports them.

Do not automatically blame the algorithm.

## 11. Weekly Experiments

Each report should recommend a small number of measurable experiments.

Examples:

- Test two different hook styles.
- Compare short and longer videos on similar topics.
- Test destination-led versus question-led openings.
- Compare practical guides with storytelling.
- Test different calls to action.

For every experiment define:

- Hypothesis.
- Variable being tested.
- Measurement.
- Comparison period.
- Success criteria.
- Result.
- Next decision.

Change one major variable at a time where practical.

## 12. Decision Rules

**REPEAT:** Evidence suggests a topic or format deserves further use.

**IMPROVE:** The idea has value but execution needs adjustment.

**TEST:** More evidence is required.

**STOP:** Repeated evidence indicates poor fit or insufficient audience value.

Avoid making permanent decisions from small samples.

## 13. Four-Week Review

Every four weeks, assess:

- Audience growth.
- Consistency.
- Strongest content pillars.
- Strongest formats.
- Audience retention patterns.
- Follower conversion.
- Search-driven discovery.
- Repeated audience questions.
- Progress towards monetisation readiness.

Identify longer-term trends rather than reacting to individual videos.

## 14. Reporting Integrity

The agent must:

- Use recorded metrics.
- State the measurement period.
- Disclose missing data.
- Avoid fabricated benchmarks.
- Avoid unsupported algorithm explanations.
- Separate observations from hypotheses.
- Keep the two brands' results separate.
- Protect account and audience privacy.

## 15. Core Principle

Measure what matters. Learn from the evidence. Make the next week's content better.
