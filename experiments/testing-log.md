
# TikTok Growth Intelligence — Experiment Testing Log

## 1. Purpose

Record controlled experiments to discover which content approaches improve growth for:

- Travel Alerts.ke
- Simo Traveller

Decisions must be based on observed performance, not assumptions.

## 2. Experiment Rules

1. Test one main variable at a time where practical.
2. Keep the target audience and content quality reasonably consistent.
3. Record the starting hypothesis before publishing.
4. Record actual results at 24 hours, 7 days and 30 days where available.
5. Do not declare a winner from one video alone.
6. Repeat promising experiments before adopting them permanently.
7. Never sacrifice factual accuracy or audience trust for views.
8. Analyse each brand separately.

## 3. Experiment Categories

- Opening hooks.
- Video duration.
- Voiceover versus text-led formats.
- Storytelling and presentation.
- Posting times.
- Captions and keywords.
- Hashtags.
- Calls to action.
- Destination and alert topics.
- Visual styles and editing.

## 5. Experiment History

Add a new record for every experiment. Never overwrite previous results.

## 6. Review Principles

- Compare similar content wherever possible.
- Consider video length when evaluating watch time.
- Distinguish views from meaningful audience growth.
- Look at shares and saves as well as likes.
- Check whether viewers are becoming followers.
- Avoid treating short-term fluctuations as reliable trends.
- Record missing metrics as unavailable, never estimate them.

## 7. Final Principle

Test deliberately. Measure consistently. Learn from evidence. Apply successful findings without compromising either brand's identity or credibility.
