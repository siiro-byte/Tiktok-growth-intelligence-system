
# Travel Alerts.ke — TikTok Brand Strategy

## 1. Brand identity

**Brand:** Travel Alerts.ke  
**Core purpose:** Help travellers understand what is happening in the travel industry, why it matters and what they should do next.

**Editorial promise:** Why and what today.

Travel Alerts.ke is an African travel intelligence and information brand. It monitors developments that affect travellers, tourism businesses and the movement of people across borders.

The brand should be useful, credible, timely and practical.

## 2. Target audience

### Primary audience
- African travellers planning international or regional trips.
- People travelling for work, education, tourism or family visits.
- African diaspora communities travelling to and from Africa.
- Frequent flyers and people using African airports and airlines.

### Secondary audience
- Travel agencies and tour operators.
- Hotels, lodges and tourism businesses.
- Travel professionals and industry observers.
- Organisations involved in tourism, aviation and border management.

## 3. Geographic coverage

### Primary coverage
All five African regions:
- East Africa
- West Africa
- Central Africa
- Southern Africa
- North Africa

### International coverage
Cover developments outside Africa when they have significant consequences for African travellers.

Prioritise:
- Middle East travel developments.
- China and major Asian travel markets.
- Europe and the United Kingdom.
- Major international aviation and border developments.

Do not restrict coverage to Kenya or any single African country.

## 4. Content pillars

### A. Border, visa and passport intelligence
- Visa changes and entry requirements.
- Border closures and restrictions.
- Passport rules and validity requirements.
- Immigration policy changes.
- Work permits and travel authorisations.
- Transit and connecting-flight requirements.

### B. Travel advisories and safety
- Government travel advisories.
- Security developments affecting destinations.
- Natural disasters and extreme weather.
- Public health developments affecting travel.
- Airport and transport disruptions.

### C. Airlines and airports
- New airline routes.
- Flight suspensions and schedule changes.
- Airport developments.
- Airline policy changes.
- Verified discounts and promotional fares.
- Passenger rights and practical travel advice.

### D. Destinations and tourism opportunities
- Places worth visiting.
- Entry fees and park charges.
- Seasonal travel opportunities.
- Sustainable tourism.
- Accommodation and hospitality developments.
- Local travel regulations.

### E. Travel industry intelligence
- Hotel and airline developments.
- Tourism investment and partnerships.
- Major travel events and conferences.
- Hospitality trends.
- Wildlife conservation and human-wildlife conflict where relevant to tourism.

## 5. Editorial standards

Every factual alert must answer as many of these questions as relevant:

1. What happened?
2. Where did it happen?
3. Who is affected?
4. When does it take effect?
5. What should travellers do?
6. What is confirmed, and what remains uncertain?

### Source hierarchy

Prioritise:
1. Government ministries and immigration authorities.
2. Civil aviation authorities and airport operators.
3. Airlines and official transport operators.
4. Tourism boards and recognised industry organisations.
5. Reputable news organisations.
6. Other sources only when independently verified.

Always provide a source or clearly identify the issuing authority.

### Accuracy rules
- Never present speculation as confirmed news.
- Distinguish a visa suspension from enhanced screening.
- Distinguish proposed policy from an implemented policy.
- State effective dates and expiry dates where available.
- Verify country lists and affected nationalities.
- Do not exaggerate risks to attract views.
- Correct material errors promptly.

## 6. TikTok content format

### Recommended video structure

**Opening (0–3 seconds):** A clear, specific hook.

**Context (3–10 seconds):** Explain the development.

**Impact (10–25 seconds):** Identify who is affected and why.

**Practical advice (25–40 seconds):** Tell viewers what to check or do.

**Closing:** Direct viewers to the issuing authority or invite a relevant question.

The duration should match the complexity of the story. Do not stretch a simple update to meet an arbitrary video length.

### Content formats
- Breaking travel alerts.
- Explainer videos.
- Country-specific travel updates.
- Airline and airport updates.
- Destination guides.
- Weekly travel intelligence round-ups.
- Myth-versus-fact explainers.

## 7. Search and discovery strategy

Use the language travellers actually search for.

Examples:
- Kenya visa requirements.
- Africa travel alerts.
- Latest visa changes.
- African passport updates.
- Flights to Africa.
- Airport delays.
- Travel restrictions.
- Best places to visit in Africa.
- Tanzania entry requirements.
- South Africa travel advisory.

Use one primary search phrase per video. Include it naturally in the spoken content, on-screen headline and caption.

Hashtags should be relevant to the actual subject, not added simply because they are popular.

## 8. Brand voice

The voice should be:
- Clear and authoritative.
- Calm, not alarmist.
- Practical and informative.
- Independent and fair.
- Accessible to ordinary travellers.

Avoid:
- Sensational headlines.
- Unnecessary jargon.
- Political commentary unrelated to travel.
- Unsupported claims.
- Excessive promotional language.

## 9. Visual identity

Use the established Travel Alerts.ke identity:

- Deep navy blue for serious alerts.
- Tangerine/orange for travel opportunities and selected highlights.
- White for readable text.
- Bold, clear typography.
- Minimal graphics.
- Strong headlines and short supporting text.

Avoid clutter, unnecessary icons and decorative elements that compete with the information.

## 10. Growth objectives

The goal is not simply to accumulate views. Build a trusted audience that returns because the information is useful.

Priorities:
1. Improve audience retention.
2. Increase saves and shares.
3. Build returning viewers.
4. Grow relevant followers across African markets.
5. Become a recognised source of practical African travel intelligence.
6. Develop opportunities for tourism partnerships and sustainable monetisation.

## 11. Performance measurement

Review each video after publication.

Track:
- Views.
- Average watch time.
- Percentage watched.
- Completion rate.
- Shares.
- Saves.
- Comments.
- Profile visits.
- Followers gained.
- Search traffic where available.

Compare videos with similar topics and formats. Avoid judging the whole strategy from one viral or unsuccessful post.

## 12. Strategic principle

**Travel Alerts.ke should answer three questions:**

- WHY should a traveller care?
- WHAT has changed?
- WHAT should the traveller do next?

A successful video leaves the viewer better informed and better prepared to make a travel decision.
