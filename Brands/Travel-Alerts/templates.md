
# Travel Alerts.ke — TikTok Production Templates

## 1. Purpose

This document provides repeatable video production templates for Travel Alerts.ke.

Every video should be:
- Accurate and properly sourced.
- Easy to understand.
- Relevant to travellers.
- Structured for strong audience retention.
- Practical enough to encourage saves and shares.

The templates are starting points. Adapt them to the actual story rather than forcing every development into the same format.

---

## 2. Breaking travel alert

**Best for:** Urgent visa changes, border closures, airport disruptions and official travel advisories.

**Recommended length:** 25–45 seconds, depending on complexity.

### Video structure

**Opening hook (0–3 seconds)**

[Country/authority] has announced an important travel update affecting [affected travellers].

**Context (3–10 seconds)**

The announcement concerns [explain the development]. It was issued on [date] and takes effect on [effective date, if confirmed].

**Impact (10–25 seconds)**

The change affects [nationalities, locations or categories of travellers].

It means [explain practical consequences].

**Action (25–35 seconds)**

If you are travelling, check [specific document, requirement or official instruction] before departure.

**Closing**

Follow Travel Alerts.ke for practical travel updates across Africa and beyond.

### On-screen text

- TRAVEL ALERT
- [COUNTRY]
- [SHORT HEADLINE]
- AFFECTED: [GROUP]
- EFFECTIVE: [DATE]
- CHECK: [ACTION]

### Caption

Travel update: [Country] has announced [development].

Who is affected: [group].

Effective date: [date or status].

What travellers should do: [practical advice].

Source: [official authority and link].

Verify the latest requirements before travelling.

#TravelAlerts #TravelNews #AfricaTravel #[Country]

## 3. Visa and passport update

**Best for:** New visa rules, entry requirements, passport validity and electronic travel authorisations.

### Voiceover structure

**Hook**

Travelling to [country]? Here is what you need to know about the latest visa update.

**Explanation**

[Authority] has announced [specific change].

The new requirement applies to [affected nationalities or traveller categories].

It begins on [date], according to the official announcement.

**Practical information**

Before departure, check:

1. Whether your nationality is affected.
2. Whether you need a visa or travel authorisation.
3. Passport validity requirements.
4. Supporting documents.
5. Whether transit passengers are covered.

**Closing**

Always confirm your individual requirements with the official immigration authority before booking or travelling.

### On-screen text

VISA UPDATE: [COUNTRY]

Who is affected?
[Nationalities/groups]

What changed?
[Short explanation]

Effective:
[Date]

### Caption

Planning to travel to [country]?

Here is the latest information on [visa/entry requirement].

Affected travellers: [group].

Effective date: [date].

Official information: [source/link].

Requirements may differ by nationality and travel purpose.

#VisaUpdate #PassportUpdate #TravelRequirements #TravelAlerts

## 11. Travel explainer

**Best for:** Evergreen educational content and common traveller questions.

### Voiceover structure

**Hook**

Do you need a transit visa if you never leave the airport?

**Explanation**

The answer depends on [nationality, airport, itinerary and destination rules].

**Example**

For a journey through [country], travellers should check [specific official rule].

**Practical checklist**

Before booking:

1. Check your nationality's requirements.
2. Confirm whether your connection is airside.
3. Check whether you need to change terminals or airports.
4. Verify the rules with the destination's official immigration authority and airline.

**Closing**

Never assume that remaining inside an airport automatically removes transit visa requirements.

### Caption

Before travelling, confirm the requirements with the relevant official authority.

## 13. Caption writing formula

Use this structure for most posts:

**First line:** Main subject and country.

**Second line:** What happened.

**Third line:** Who is affected.

**Fourth line:** Practical advice.

**Fifth line:** Source or official link.

**Final line:** Relevant hashtags.

Keep captions useful and readable. Avoid repeating the entire voiceover word for word.

## 14. Call-to-action library

Choose one relevant CTA per video.

### Follow
- Follow Travel Alerts.ke for practical travel updates.
- Follow us for African travel intelligence.

### Save
- Save this update before your next trip.
- Keep this information handy when planning your journey.

### Share
- Share this with someone travelling to [country].
- Send this to a traveller who may be affected.

### Comment
- Which country should we investigate next?
- Have you experienced this at the border?
- What travel question should we explain next?

Avoid using unrelated engagement prompts.

Use a small number of relevant hashtags.

Recommended approach:
- One brand or broad travel hashtag.
- One subject-specific hashtag.
- One country or regional hashtag.
- One relevant discovery hashtag.

## 16. Research and publishing checklist

Before recording:

- [ ] Identify the original announcement.
- [ ] Confirm the publishing date.
- [ ] Confirm the effective date, if applicable.
- [ ] Identify affected countries and nationalities.
- [ ] Verify the practical implications.
- [ ] Separate confirmed facts from analysis.
- [ ] Identify an authoritative source.
- [ ] Select an appropriate video template.

Before publishing:

- [ ] The opening accurately describes the story.
- [ ] On-screen text is readable.
- [ ] Names, dates and figures are correct.
- [ ] Caption includes practical information.
- [ ] Source is identified.
- [ ] Hashtags are relevant.
- [ ] CTA matches the subject.
- [ ] No private or sensitive traveller information is exposed.

After publishing:

- [ ] Record the video URL and publication date.
- [ ] Track watch time and retention.
- [ ] Record saves, shares and comments.
- [ ] Note questions that need follow-up.
- [ ] Correct material errors promptly.

---

## 17. Final production standard

Every Travel Alerts.ke video should deliver:

1. A clear opening.
2. A verified development or useful travel explanation.
3. The people, places or journeys affected.
4. Practical information.
5. An identifiable source.

**The objective is not simply to publish more videos. It is to become a trusted source that travellers actively seek out.**
