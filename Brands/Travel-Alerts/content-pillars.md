
# Travel Alerts.ke — TikTok Content Pillars and Publishing System

## 1. Purpose

This document translates the Travel Alerts.ke brand strategy into a repeatable TikTok publishing system.

The objective is to publish useful, timely and credible travel intelligence that attracts the right audience, earns trust and encourages viewers to return.

The account should not become a page that simply reposts headlines. Every post should explain why a development matters to travellers.

## 2. Core content pillars

### Pillar 1: Border, visa and passport updates

**Priority: Very high**

Topics:
- Visa policy changes.
- Entry restrictions and suspensions.
- Passport validity requirements.
- Electronic travel authorisations.
- Work and residence permits.
- Transit visa requirements.
- Border closures and reopening.
- Immigration and customs changes.

Audience value:
Help travellers avoid denied boarding, entry problems, unexpected costs and unnecessary delays.

Recommended format:
- Country-specific alerts.
- Who is affected explainers.
- Before-you-travel checklists.
- Myth-versus-fact videos.

### Pillar 2: Travel advisories and safety

**Priority: Very high**

Topics:
- Official government travel advisories.
- Security incidents affecting travel.
- Political unrest and border disruptions.
- Natural disasters and extreme weather.
- Health-related travel restrictions.
- Evacuation and transport updates.

Audience value:
Help travellers assess risks and make informed decisions.

Recommended format:
- What happened and where.
- Areas affected.
- Official advice.
- Practical steps for travellers.

Do not use sensational language or imply that an entire country is unsafe because of an isolated incident.

### Pillar 3: Airlines, airports and flight developments

**Priority: High**

Topics:
- New African and international flight routes.
- Route suspensions and resumptions.
- Airline schedule changes.
- Airport expansion and terminal changes.
- Passenger rights and baggage rules.
- Flight disruption information.
- Verified promotional fares and discount codes.

Audience value:
Help travellers plan journeys, understand changes and identify opportunities.

Recommended format:
- New route announcements.
- Airline comparison explainers.
- Airport guides.
- Fare and promotion alerts.

For discounts, verify the booking dates, travel dates, eligible routes, conditions and expiry date before publishing.

### Pillar 4: African destinations and tourism opportunities

**Priority: High**

Topics:
- Places to visit across Africa.
- National parks and conservation areas.
- Entry fees and visitor requirements.
- Cultural and heritage destinations.
- Seasonal travel opportunities.
- Accommodation and hospitality developments.
- Sustainable tourism initiatives.

Audience value:
Inspire travel while providing the practical information needed to plan it.

Recommended format:
- Destination spotlight.
- What it costs to visit.
- Best time to travel.
- Entry requirements.
- Practical visitor tips.

Never present estimated prices as confirmed current prices.

### Pillar 5: Travel industry intelligence

**Priority: Medium**

Topics:
- Hotel and lodge developments.
- Tourism investment.
- Hospitality trends.
- Tourism board announcements.
- Aviation and travel industry partnerships.
- Major travel events.
- Conservation and human-wildlife conflict affecting tourism.

Audience value:
Help travellers and tourism professionals understand developments shaping the industry.

Recommended format:
- What the announcement means.
- Who benefits or is affected.
- What it could mean for tourism.
- What remains uncertain.

Separate confirmed developments from analysis and opinion.

## 3. Recommended publishing mix

Use the following as an initial testing allocation, not a permanent rule.

| Content category | Share |
|---|---:|
| Border, visa and passport updates | 25% |
| Travel advisories and safety | 20% |
| Airlines and airports | 20% |
| Destinations and tourism | 25% |
| Travel industry intelligence | 10% |

Adjust the mix using actual audience response and the importance of current developments.

A serious travel alert should never be delayed simply to maintain a content quota.

## 4. Weekly publishing framework

This is a starting schedule. It can change when major developments occur.

| Day | Suggested focus |
|---|---|
| Monday | Border, visa and passport developments |
| Tuesday | Airline and airport updates |
| Wednesday | African destination spotlight |
| Thursday | Travel advisory or safety explainer |
| Friday | New routes, fares and travel opportunities |
| Saturday | Destination guide or practical travel tips |
| Sunday | Weekly African travel intelligence roundup |

Publish fewer, stronger updates rather than filling the account with repetitive material.

## 5. Breaking news versus evergreen content

### Breaking and time-sensitive content

Examples:
- A new visa restriction.
- An airport closure.
- A new travel advisory.
- An airline route suspension.
- A border reopening.

Rules:
1. Verify the original announcement.
2. Confirm the date and effective period.
3. Identify affected travellers.
4. Explain the practical consequences.
5. Include an authoritative source.
6. Update or correct the post if information changes.

### Evergreen content

Examples:
- How to check visa requirements.
- What passport validity means.
- How transit visas work.
- How to prepare for an international flight.
- How to avoid common border problems.
- Planning a first African safari.

Evergreen videos help attract search traffic after the original publication date.

## 6. Geographic coverage system

Avoid concentrating coverage in one country or region.

Monitor developments across:

- East Africa: Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, South Sudan, Ethiopia and neighbouring markets.
- West Africa: Nigeria, Ghana, Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire and other regional markets.
- Central Africa: Democratic Republic of Congo, Republic of Congo, Cameroon, Gabon and neighbouring markets.
- Southern Africa: South Africa, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Botswana, Namibia, Malawi, Mozambique and neighbouring markets.
- North Africa: Egypt, Morocco, Tunisia, Algeria and neighbouring markets.

This is a monitoring guide, not a fixed list of countries.

Cover other African countries whenever a relevant development emerges.

International developments should be prioritised when they materially affect African travellers.

## 7. Content selection scorecard

Before producing a video, assess:

| Question | Score |
|---|---:|
| Does it affect travellers? | 0–2 |
| Is the information verified? | 0–2 |
| Is it timely or useful? | 0–2 |
| Can we explain the practical impact? | 0–2 |
| Is there a clear audience search or interest? | 0–2 |

Maximum score: 10.

Use this as an editorial aid:
- 8–10: Strong candidate.
- 6–7: Consider if the information is important.
- 0–5: Research further or do not publish.

An urgent, verified safety or border alert may take priority regardless of its score.

## 8. Recommended video formats

### Format A: Breaking travel alert

1. Direct headline.
2. What happened.
3. Countries or travellers affected.
4. Effective date.
5. Practical action.
6. Official source.

### Format B: Travel explainer

1. Start with a common traveller question.
2. Explain the rule or development.
3. Give a practical example.
4. Identify common mistakes.
5. Direct viewers to the relevant authority.

### Format C: Destination guide

1. Introduce the destination.
2. Explain why it is worth visiting.
3. Cover entry requirements.
4. Include verified fees and transport information.
5. Explain the best time to visit.
6. Provide practical visitor advice.

### Format D: Industry development

1. Explain the announcement.
2. Identify the organisation involved.
3. Explain what is changing.
4. Discuss likely implications.
5. Distinguish confirmed facts from analysis.

## 9. Audience engagement

Use questions that encourage useful discussion.

Examples:
- Have you travelled through this border recently?
- Which African country should we cover next?
- Have you experienced this airline policy?
- What do travellers need to know before visiting this destination?
- Which visa process should we explain next?

Do not ask viewers to share sensitive personal information such as passport numbers, visa documents or private travel details.

Respond to genuine questions and correct misinformation respectfully.

## 10. Avoiding repetitive content

Before creating a new post:

1. Check recent posts on the account.
2. Search for previous coverage of the same development.
3. Determine whether there is genuinely new information.
4. Identify a new audience question or practical angle.
5. Update an existing story when appropriate instead of repackaging old news as breaking news.

A topic can be revisited when there is:
- A new official announcement.
- A change in effective dates.
- New affected countries or nationalities.
- A significant clarification.
- A useful follow-up explaining what travellers should do.

## 11. Performance review

Review performance weekly.

Track each content pillar separately.

Measure:
- Average watch time.
- Completion rate.
- Shares.
- Saves.
- Comments.
- Profile visits.
- Followers gained.
- Search traffic, where available.

Questions for the weekly review:
1. Which pillar attracted the most relevant viewers?
2. Which videos generated the most saves and shares?
3. Which topics brought new followers?
4. Where did viewers stop watching?
5. Which questions appeared repeatedly in comments?
6. What should we test next week?

Do not confuse high views with high-quality audience growth.

## 12. Editorial principle

Every Travel Alerts.ke video must deliver at least one of these benefits:

- Warn travellers about a verified development.
- Explain a change in travel requirements.
- Help someone make a better travel decision.
- Reveal a useful destination or travel opportunity.
- Explain an important development in the African travel industry.

**The standard is simple: useful information, credible sources and practical consequences.**
