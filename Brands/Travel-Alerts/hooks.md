
# Travel Alerts.ke — TikTok Hook Library

## 1. Purpose

This document provides reusable opening lines for Travel Alerts.ke TikTok videos.

The goal is to capture attention in the first 1–3 seconds, communicate the subject immediately and encourage viewers to stay for the practical information.

A strong hook should:
- Identify the subject clearly.
- Create genuine interest.
- Explain why the viewer should care.
- Match the facts presented in the video.
- Avoid unnecessary sensationalism.

Do not use a hook that promises information the video does not deliver.

## 2. Visa and passport updates

Use these when immigration rules, visa requirements or passport policies change.

1. Travelling to [country]? There is an important visa update.
2. [Country] has changed its entry requirements. Here's what travellers need to know.
3. Before booking your flight to [country], check this requirement.
4. African travellers heading to [country] should take note of this update.
5. Your passport may need more than just a valid travel date.
6. Planning to work in [country]? Understand this change before travelling.
7. This is what the latest visa announcement means for travellers.
8. Travelling through [country]? Your transit requirements matter.
9. Here are the nationalities affected by the new entry rules.
10. A visa approval does not always guarantee entry. Here's why.

## 6. Flight deals and travel opportunities

Only use when a promotion or fare has been verified and is still valid.

1. Looking for a cheaper way to fly to [destination]?
2. [Airline] is offering a promotion on selected routes.
3. Before using this flight discount, check the conditions.
4. Here are the routes included in this travel offer.
5. Is this flight deal actually worth booking?
6. Travelling during [season]? Compare these available options.
7. This promotion has a limited booking window. Here's what to check.
8. Want to save on your next flight? Start with these details.

Always include:
- Booking period.
- Travel period.
- Eligible routes.
- Discount code, if applicable.
- Restrictions and expiry date.
- Official booking source.

Never suggest that a fare is available to everyone if eligibility is restricted.

## 13. Hook writing formulas

### Formula A: Audience + development
"[Audience] travelling to [destination]? Here's what changed."

### Formula B: Question + practical answer
"Do you need a transit visa in [country]? Here's how to check."

### Formula C: Announcement + impact
"[Authority/airline] has announced [change]. Here's who is affected."

### Formula D: Common mistake + solution
"Don't assume your visa covers every transit journey. Check this first."

### Formula E: Destination + value
"Planning a trip to [destination]? Here are the costs and entry requirements to check."

### Formula F: Comparison + clarification
"Visa suspension and enhanced screening are not the same thing. Here's the difference."

## 14. Hook quality checklist

Before publishing, ask:

- [ ] Does the first sentence identify the subject?
- [ ] Is the hook relevant to the intended audience?
- [ ] Is the statement factually supported?
- [ ] Does the video deliver what the hook promises?
- [ ] Is the wording clear when spoken aloud?
- [ ] Can the viewer understand the topic without reading a long caption?
- [ ] Have we avoided unnecessary fear or exaggeration?

If the hook is misleading, rewrite it.

## 15. Testing different hooks

When a topic allows more than one truthful approach, test different openings.

Example: A new visa requirement for travellers to [country].

**Version A — Direct**
"[Country] has introduced a new visa requirement."

**Version B — Audience-focused**
"Travelling to [country]? Check this new requirement."

**Version C — Practical**
"Before booking your trip to [country], understand this entry rule."

Compare:
- Average watch time.
- Early viewer retention.
- Completion rate.
- Shares and saves.
- Relevant comments.
- Followers gained.

Test one major variable at a time where possible. Do not assume one hook will work equally well for every subject.

## 16. Important editorial rule

Travel Alerts.ke is an information brand, not a clickbait account.

The strongest hook is not necessarily the loudest.

It is the opening that makes the right traveller stop, understand the issue and want the practical explanation.

**Our standard: Clear headline. Verified development. Practical value.**
