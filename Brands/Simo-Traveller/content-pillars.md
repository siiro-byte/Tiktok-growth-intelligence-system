
# Simo Traveller — Content Pillars

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the main content categories for Simo Traveller and provides a framework for choosing, organising and publishing videos.

The objective is to build a recognisable African travel brand that inspires discovery, provides practical information and develops a loyal audience.

Every video should serve a clear audience need.

## 2. The Five Core Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Destination Discovery

**Purpose:** Introduce viewers to interesting destinations and inspire them to explore.

Topics:
- Hidden gems in Kenya.
- Underrated destinations in Africa.
- Beautiful towns and landscapes.
- Places worth visiting during holidays.
- Lesser-known attractions.
- Unique natural and cultural experiences.

Example video ideas:
- 5 places in Kenya worth discovering.
- A beautiful destination many travellers overlook.
- Why you should consider visiting this part of Africa.
- What makes this destination special?

### Pillar 2: Wildlife and Safari

**Purpose:** Showcase Africa's wildlife, safari experiences and natural heritage.

Topics:
- National parks and wildlife reserves.
- Safari experiences.
- Wildlife behaviour and encounters.
- Conservation initiatives.
- Responsible tourism.
- Human-wildlife coexistence.

Example video ideas:
- What makes Amboseli special?
- Wildlife you can encounter in Kenya.
- Understanding conservation beyond the safari.
- What to know before visiting a national park.

### Pillar 3: Hiking and Outdoor Adventures

**Purpose:** Encourage outdoor exploration and responsible adventure.

Topics:
- Hiking destinations.
- Mountains and forests.
- Camping locations.
- Nature trails.
- Outdoor preparation.
- Safety and responsible exploration.

Example video ideas:
- Is this one of Kenya's most interesting hiking destinations?
- What to prepare before hiking Mount Suswa.
- Exploring Kenya's forests.
- Places to go camping near Nairobi.

### Pillar 4: Practical Travel Guides

**Purpose:** Help viewers make informed travel decisions.

Topics:
- How to reach destinations.
- Public transport and road journeys.
- Accommodation options.
- Entry fees and visitor requirements.
- Travel budgets.
- Best times to visit.
- Packing and preparation.
- Booking and planning advice.

Example video ideas:
- How to get to Watamu from Nairobi.
- What a first-time visitor should know about Maasai Mara.
- Where to stay when visiting a destination.
- How to plan a weekend getaway.

Practical information must be checked before publication. Clearly distinguish confirmed prices from estimates.

### Pillar 5: Travel Stories and Experiences

**Purpose:** Build an emotional connection with viewers through storytelling.

Topics:
- Journeys and road trips.
- Personal travel experiences.
- Unexpected discoveries.
- Local people and culture.
- Lessons from travelling.
- Scenic moments and memorable experiences.

Example video ideas:
- Sometimes the journey is the destination.
- What travelling through this region taught me.
- A place that deserves more attention.
- The moments that make travel worthwhile.

Only present a story as first-hand experience when it genuinely happened.

## 3. Recommended Content Mix

Starting allocation for every 20 published videos:

| Content pillar | Suggested videos |
|---|---:|
| Destination discovery | 5 |
| Practical travel guides | 5 |
| Wildlife and safari | 4 |
| Hiking and outdoor adventures | 3 |
| Travel stories and experiences | 3 |
| **Total** | **20** |

This is a starting hypothesis, not a permanent rule.

Review performance after a meaningful sample of published videos. Increase the categories that attract engaged viewers, saves, shares and returning audiences.

## 4. Recurring Content Series

Recurring series help viewers recognise the channel and give them reasons to return.

### Series A: Discover Kenya

Explore destinations across Kenya.

Possible episodes:
- Discover the Kenyan coast.
- Discover Kenya's hidden gems.
- Discover Kenya's national parks.
- Discover Kenya's mountains and forests.

### Series B: Africa Beyond the Headlines

Explore African destinations through scenery, culture, nature and practical travel information.

This is a travel discovery series, not a breaking-news or political commentary format.

### Series C: Before You Visit

Short practical guides answering important questions before someone travels.

Possible episodes:
- What to know before visiting Maasai Mara.
- What to prepare before hiking Mount Kipipiri.
- What to know before travelling to Zanzibar.
- What to consider before booking a safari.

### Series D: The Journey

Show the route, transport, scenery and experience of travelling between destinations.

Possible episodes:
- Nairobi to the Kenyan coast.
- Exploring destinations by train.
- Road trips across Kenya.
- Cross-border journeys in Africa.

### Series E: Wild Africa

Wildlife, conservation, national parks and safari experiences.

### Series F: Let's Go Hiking

Focus on mountains, forests, trails, camping and outdoor exploration.

Feature responsible hiking and conservation, including destinations such as Mount Kipipiri and the Aberdare Forest.

## 5. Platform Strategy

### TikTok

Primary role:
- Discovery.
- Short destination features.
- Quick travel advice.
- Engaging travel stories.
- Testing new topics and hooks.

Recommended formats:
- 15–60 second videos.
- Scenery with voiceover.
- Strong opening hooks.
- Clear on-screen destination names.
- One useful message per video.

### YouTube Shorts

Primary role:
- Reach new viewers.
- Introduce destinations.
- Build recognition.
- Direct viewers to relevant longer videos.

Repurpose suitable short videos, but adapt titles, descriptions and presentation to YouTube.

### YouTube Long-form

Primary role:
- Build a loyal audience.
- Develop deeper destination coverage.
- Provide useful travel guides.
- Create long-term monetisation opportunities.

Recommended formats:
- Complete destination guides.
- Travel documentaries.
- Road-trip journeys.
- Safari and hiking experiences.
- Accommodation reviews.
- Detailed travel planning guides.

## 6. Topic Selection Framework

Before producing a video, evaluate the topic against these questions:

1. Is it relevant to Kenya or African travel?
2. Does it offer something useful, interesting or inspiring?
3. Is there a clear audience likely to care?
4. Can we produce it with available footage or reliable research?
5. Does it fit one of the five content pillars?
6. Can the information be verified?

Prioritise topics that answer several of these questions strongly.

## 7. Content Formats to Rotate

Avoid making every video look or sound the same.

Rotate between:

- Scenic destination introductions.
- Educational voiceovers.
- Step-by-step travel guides.
- Short travel stories.
- Wildlife features.
- Transport and route explainers.
- Accommodation features.
- Destination comparisons.
- Travel preparation tips.
- Longer journey documentaries.

The main identity remains scenery, voiceover and storytelling.

## 8. Publishing Framework

A practical starting weekly schedule:

| Day | Suggested content |
|---|---|
| Monday | Destination discovery |
| Tuesday | Practical travel guide |
| Wednesday | Wildlife or nature |
| Thursday | Hiking or outdoor adventure |
| Friday | Travel story |
| Weekend | Strongest topic, destination feature or longer video |

This is a flexible planning framework, not a requirement to publish every day.

Consistency and quality matter more than filling a calendar.

## 9. Avoiding Repetitive Content

Before publishing, check whether the proposed video adds a new perspective.

If the destination has already been covered, consider a different angle:

- A new route.
- A different experience.
- Practical costs.
- Accommodation.
- Best time to visit.
- Visitor requirements.
- Conservation.
- Local culture.
- A personal experience.
- A comparison with another destination.

Do not repeat the same footage, hook and information simply to maintain posting frequency.

## 10. Audience Engagement

Give viewers a reason to participate.

Examples:
- Which Kenyan destination should we explore next?
- Have you visited this place?
- Would you choose a safari or a hiking adventure?
- What should first-time visitors know?
- Which African country should feature next?

Use genuine questions that encourage useful discussion.

Respond to meaningful comments and consider turning recurring questions into new videos.

## 11. Performance Review

Evaluate each content pillar separately.

### Discovery
- Views.
- Reach.
- New followers.
- Profile visits.

### Engagement
- Watch time.
- Completion rate.
- Shares.
- Saves.
- Comments.

### Audience loyalty
- Returning viewers.
- Repeat engagement.
- Subscribers gained.
- Viewers watching multiple videos.

### Business potential
- Questions about destinations.
- Accommodation enquiries.
- Partnership interest.
- Affiliate link engagement.

Do not judge a content category from one video. Look for patterns across multiple publications.

## 12. Strategic Principle

Simo Traveller should not become just another collection of beautiful travel clips.

Every destination should have a story. Every guide should answer a real question. Every experience should give the viewer a reason to discover more.

**The goal: Build an audience that does not just watch Simo Traveller, but returns to explore Africa with Simo Traveller.**
