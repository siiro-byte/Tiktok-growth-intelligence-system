
# Simo Traveller — Brand Strategy

## 1. Brand Identity

**Brand name:** Simo Traveller  
**Primary platform:** YouTube  
**Supporting platform:** TikTok  
**Language:** English  
**Geographic focus:** Kenya and Africa  
**Creator:** Kenyan travel creator

Simo Traveller is a travel discovery and storytelling brand showcasing places, experiences and practical ways to explore Kenya and Africa.

The brand combines scenic storytelling with useful travel information to help people discover destinations, plan journeys and experience more of Africa.

**Core idea: Discover more. Travel smarter. Experience Africa.**

## 2. Brand Positioning

Simo Traveller makes travel more accessible, interesting and understandable.

The content should do more than show beautiful scenery. It should help viewers understand:

- Where a destination is.
- Why it is worth visiting.
- How to get there.
- What visitors can experience.
- What practical requirements to consider.
- What a traveller should know before going.

The brand should develop a recognisable identity built around authentic travel discovery, useful information and engaging storytelling.

### Difference from Travel Alerts.ke

- **Simo Traveller:** Shows people where to go, what to experience and how to explore.
- **Travel Alerts.ke:** Explains travel developments, risks, regulations and information that travellers need to know.

The brands may cover related subjects, but they must retain separate audiences, voices and editorial approaches.

## 3. Target Audience

Primary audiences:

- Kenyans interested in exploring their country.
- Africans interested in discovering destinations across the continent.
- Budget-conscious and mid-range travellers.
- Solo travellers and independent explorers.
- Wildlife, safari and nature enthusiasts.
- Hikers, campers and outdoor adventure enthusiasts.
- International viewers interested in African destinations.

The content should be accessible to both experienced travellers and people planning their first trips.

## 4. Geographic Coverage

### Primary focus: Kenya

Showcase destinations, including:

- National parks and wildlife reserves.
- Beaches and coastal destinations.
- Mountains, forests and hiking trails.
- Lakes, landscapes and scenic locations.
- Small towns and lesser-known destinations.
- Cultural and nature-based experiences.

### Secondary focus: Africa

Explore destinations across Africa, including:

- Tanzania.
- Uganda.
- Rwanda.
- Zambia.
- Zimbabwe.
- Botswana.
- Namibia.
- Other African destinations as opportunities arise.

International destinations may be included where they support the brand's travel discovery direction.

## 5. Content Pillars

### A. Destination Discovery

Introduce interesting destinations and explain what makes them worth visiting.

Examples:
- Hidden gems in Kenya.
- Places to visit in Tanzania.
- Underrated African destinations.
- What makes a destination special.

### B. Wildlife and Safaris

Showcase Africa's wildlife and natural heritage.

Examples:
- National park experiences.
- Wildlife encounters.
- Safari destination guides.
- Conservation and human-wildlife coexistence.
- Responsible wildlife tourism.

### C. Hiking and Outdoor Adventures

Cover hiking, camping, forests, mountains and outdoor experiences.

Examples:
- Hiking trails.
- Mountain adventures.
- Camping destinations.
- What to prepare before a hike.
- Outdoor safety and responsible exploration.

### D. Practical Travel Guides

Help viewers plan real journeys.

Examples:
- How to reach a destination.
- Transport options.
- Accommodation choices.
- Entry requirements and fees.
- Travel budgets and cost considerations.
- Best times to visit.
- What to pack and prepare.

### E. Travel Stories and Experiences

Create memorable stories around places, people and journeys.

Examples:
- A journey through a destination.
- What makes a place memorable.
- Unexpected discoveries.
- Local experiences.
- Lessons from travelling.

## 6. Content Style and Personality

Simo Traveller should feel:

- Curious.
- Authentic.
- Informative.
- Adventurous.
- Approachable.
- Visually engaging.

### Preferred presentation

The main creative format is:

**Scenery + voiceover + storytelling.**

Use destination footage, natural landscapes, wildlife, transport scenes and relevant supporting visuals.

Voiceovers should add context and meaning rather than simply describe what viewers can already see.

Avoid unnecessary talking-head presentations. On-camera appearances may be used when they add value to the story.

## 7. Content Formats

### Short-form videos

Primary purpose:
- Reach new audiences.
- Introduce destinations.
- Share useful travel tips.
- Create curiosity.
- Direct interested viewers towards longer guides.

Recommended formats:
- 15–60 second destination features.
- Short travel stories.
- Quick practical guides.
- Scenic videos with informative voiceovers.
- Multi-part destination series.

### Long-form YouTube videos

Primary purpose:
- Build trust.
- Develop loyal viewers.
- Provide detailed travel information.
- Create opportunities for long-term monetisation.

Recommended formats:
- Destination guides.
- Complete travel itineraries.
- Transport and accommodation guides.
- Safari and hiking experiences.
- Journey documentaries.
- Honest destination reviews.

Short videos should complement long-form content rather than replace it.

## 8. Search and Discoverability

Content should be designed around what travellers actually search for.

Examples:
- Places to visit in Kenya.
- Best hiking destinations in Kenya.
- How to visit Mount Suswa.
- Affordable safari destinations in Africa.
- Things to do in Watamu.
- How to travel from Nairobi to a destination.

Use clear destination names and relevant search phrases in:

- Video titles.
- Opening voiceovers.
- On-screen text.
- Captions and descriptions.
- Relevant hashtags.

Prioritise natural language and useful information over hashtag stuffing.

## 9. Trust and Editorial Standards

Trust is a long-term brand asset.

Simo Traveller must:

1. Never claim to have visited a destination unless the creator has actually visited it.
2. Distinguish first-hand experiences from research-based destination guides.
3. Verify travel requirements, entry fees and transport information before publishing.
4. State when prices are estimates and identify when they were checked.
5. Avoid presenting outdated information as current.
6. Be honest about sponsored trips, partnerships and affiliate links.
7. Avoid misleading destination footage or exaggerated claims.
8. Respect local communities, wildlife and conservation rules.

When information cannot be verified, explain the uncertainty rather than inventing an answer.

## 10. Audience Growth Strategy

The objective is not simply to accumulate views. It is to develop an audience that returns because it trusts and enjoys the content.

Priorities:

- Build recognisable content series.
- Give viewers a reason to follow.
- Encourage meaningful comments and questions.
- Use destination guides to attract search traffic.
- Connect short-form videos to relevant long-form guides.
- Study returning viewers and repeat engagement.
- Improve storytelling through consistent experimentation.

Avoid chasing every trend if it does not fit the brand.

## 11. Monetisation and Business Development

Long-term opportunities include:

### YouTube revenue
Build an eligible audience and work towards YouTube Partner Programme requirements.

### Accommodation partnerships
Work with hotels, lodges, camps and other tourism businesses to produce honest destination and accommodation features.

### Affiliate marketing
Use relevant accommodation, travel booking and equipment affiliate programmes, with transparent disclosures.

### Tourism partnerships
Explore collaborations with tourism boards, destination marketing organisations, conservation organisations and tourism associations.

### Travel services
Develop opportunities for travel planning, curated experiences, destination guides and other tourism-related services as the brand grows.

Partnerships must support audience trust and provide genuine value.

## 12. Performance Measurement

Evaluate content using more than views.

### Short-form metrics
- Average watch time.
- Completion rate.
- Rewatches.
- Shares.
- Saves.
- Comments.
- Profile visits.
- Followers gained per video.

### YouTube metrics
- Click-through rate.
- Average view duration.
- Returning viewers.
- Subscriber conversion.
- Search traffic.
- Watch time.
- Audience retention.

Review performance regularly and identify which topics, hooks and storytelling formats attract the right audience.

## 13. Strategic Principle

Every Simo Traveller video should achieve at least one of three things:

1. Inspire someone to discover a destination.
2. Help someone plan a journey.
3. Give someone a memorable travel experience through storytelling.

The long-term ambition is to build a trusted African travel media brand that can grow into a sustainable travel business.

**Simo Traveller: Discover more. Travel smarter. Experience Africa.**
