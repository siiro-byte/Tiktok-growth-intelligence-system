
# Simo Traveller — Video Hooks Library

## 1. Purpose

This file contains reusable opening hooks for Simo Traveller videos.

The objective is to capture attention quickly, create curiosity and give viewers a reason to continue watching.

A good hook should:
- Identify the destination or subject.
- Create curiosity or communicate value.
- Sound natural when spoken.
- Match the actual content.
- Avoid exaggerated or misleading claims.

The preferred presentation is scenery supported by engaging voiceovers.

## 13. Hook Formulas

Use these formulas to create original hooks for new topics.

### Formula A: Destination + curiosity

"Have you discovered [destination]? Here is what makes it interesting."

### Formula B: Audience + problem

"Planning to visit [destination]? Here is what you need to know."

### Formula C: Unexpected discovery

"Most people know [popular destination], but have you considered [alternative]?"

Only use comparisons when the information supports them.

### Formula D: Practical value

"Before travelling to [destination], understand these [number] things."

### Formula E: Journey

"Travelling from [origin] to [destination]? Let's look at the route."

### Formula F: Personal story

"I visited [destination] and discovered [genuine experience]."

Only use this formula when the creator actually visited.

## 14. Choosing the Right Hook

| Video type | Best hook approach |
|---|---|
| Destination feature | Curiosity and discovery |
| Safari | Wildlife and experience |
| Hiking | Adventure and preparation |
| Travel guide | Practical value |
| Transport | Route and convenience |
| Accommodation | Visitor needs and property features |
| Personal story | Genuine experience |
| Documentary | Storytelling and context |

## 15. Hook Testing

For selected topics, create two or three different openings while keeping the main video information consistent.

Compare:
- Average watch time.
- First-seconds retention, where available.
- Completion rate.
- Rewatches.
- Shares and saves.
- Comments and follows.

Do not declare a winning hook from a single upload. Look for repeatable patterns across comparable videos.

## 16. Editorial Rules

Never:
- Invent personal experiences.
- Claim a destination is the best without evidence.
- Use fake urgency.
- Promise experiences that cannot be guaranteed.
- Use misleading footage.
- Exaggerate prices or travel conditions.
- Make a hook that the video does not fulfil.

A strong opening earns attention. Useful content earns trust.

## 17. Core Principle

The first few seconds should make viewers curious about the destination, the journey or the information being shared.

But the real objective is not simply to stop someone scrolling.

**It is to give them a reason to stay, learn something and return to Simo Traveller.**
