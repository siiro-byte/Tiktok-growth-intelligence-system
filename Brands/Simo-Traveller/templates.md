
# Simo Traveller — Video Production Templates

## 1. Purpose

This document provides reusable production templates for Simo Traveller.

It covers:
- TikTok videos.
- YouTube Shorts.
- YouTube long-form videos.
- Destination guides.
- Travel storytelling.
- Wildlife and safari features.
- Hiking and outdoor adventures.
- Transport and accommodation guides.

The primary creative identity is:

**Scenery + voiceover + storytelling + useful travel information.**

Every video should inspire discovery, answer a question or tell a meaningful story.

## 2. Standard Short-Form Video Structure

Recommended starting duration: 20–60 seconds.

### Scene 1 — Hook (0–3 seconds)

Capture attention with a strong opening statement or question.

### Scene 2 — Introduce the destination (3–10 seconds)

Show where the destination is and introduce its main attraction.

### Scene 3 — Build the story (10–30 seconds)

Show scenery, wildlife, activities or other relevant experiences.

### Scene 4 — Provide value (30–45 seconds)

Share a useful fact, travel tip, practical detail or interesting observation.

### Scene 5 — Call to action (final seconds)

Encourage viewers to save, comment, follow or explore another video.

Adapt the structure to the subject. Do not force every video to use the same sequence.

## 3. Destination Discovery Template

**Objective:** Introduce a destination and inspire viewers to explore it.

### Production brief

- Destination: [Name]
- Country: [Country]
- Main attraction: [Attraction]
- Target audience: [Audience]
- Video duration: [Duration]
- Available footage: [Footage description]

### Voiceover structure

**Opening:**

"Looking for somewhere interesting to explore? Let me introduce you to [destination]."

**Introduction:**

"Located in [location], this destination is known for [verified distinguishing feature]."

**Experience:**

"Visitors can explore [activity or attraction], enjoy [experience] and discover [another feature]."

**Practical information:**

"Before visiting, consider [important travel information]."

**Closing:**

"Would you add [destination] to your travel list? Follow Simo Traveller for more African travel discoveries."

### Visual sequence

1. Best scenic shot.
2. Wide destination view.
3. Main attraction.
4. Supporting scenery or activities.
5. Closing scenic shot.

## 4. Kenya Destination Guide Template

**Objective:** Help viewers discover places within Kenya.

### Video structure

1. Hook: Introduce the destination.
2. Location: Explain where it is.
3. Attractions: Highlight what makes it interesting.
4. Activities: Explain what visitors can do.
5. Practical advice: Transport, fees, timing or preparation.
6. CTA: Encourage viewers to save or share.

### Example opening

"Kenya has so much more to offer beyond its famous destinations. Today, let's discover [destination]."

### Research checklist

- Confirm destination location.
- Identify official attractions.
- Check visitor access.
- Verify entry fees where applicable.
- Check transport information.
- Identify relevant visitor restrictions.
- Verify the date of any prices or requirements.

## 5. African Destination Guide Template

**Objective:** Introduce destinations outside Kenya to an African and international audience.

### Voiceover structure

**Opening:**

"Today, we are discovering another remarkable destination in Africa: [destination]."

**Location:**

"[Destination] is located in [country/region]."

**What makes it special:**

"It is known for [verified attraction, landscape, wildlife or cultural experience]."

**What visitors can do:**

"Depending on the season and access arrangements, visitors can explore [activities]."

**Practical details:**

"Before travelling, check [entry requirements, transport, fees and local guidance]."

**Closing:**

"Which African destination should we explore next?"

Do not assume that entry requirements, costs or visitor access are the same across African countries.

## 6. Wildlife and Safari Template

**Objective:** Tell a story about wildlife, safari destinations or conservation.

### Voiceover structure

**Hook:**

"There is more to a safari than simply seeing wildlife."

**Context:**

"Today, we are exploring [park/reserve] in [country]."

**Wildlife:**

"This area is known for [verified wildlife species or ecosystem]."

**Experience:**

"Visitors may experience [activities or wildlife viewing opportunities], subject to conditions and park rules."

**Conservation:**

"Protecting these habitats is important for [relevant ecological reason]."

**Closing:**

"Would you include this destination in your next safari?"

### Visual guidance

- Landscape establishing shots.
- Wildlife footage.
- Natural animal behaviour.
- Habitat and vegetation.
- Park or reserve identification.
- Conservation-related visuals where relevant.

Never imply that an animal sighting is guaranteed.

## 7. Hiking and Outdoor Adventure Template

**Objective:** Introduce a hiking trail, mountain, forest or outdoor experience.

### Voiceover structure

**Hook:**

"Looking for your next outdoor adventure in Kenya?"

**Introduction:**

"Today, we are exploring [trail/destination], located in [location]."

**Experience:**

"The area offers [verified landscape, trail or natural features]."

**Preparation:**

"Before attempting this adventure, consider [fitness, equipment, weather, guide requirements and safety]."

**Responsible exploration:**

"Respect the environment, follow local guidance and leave no litter behind."

**Closing:**

"Would you take on this adventure? Follow Simo Traveller for more outdoor discoveries."

### Research checklist

- Trail location.
- Route and distance.
- Elevation and difficulty, if verified.
- Access arrangements.
- Guide requirements.
- Weather considerations.
- Safety information.
- Conservation restrictions.

Do not invent hiking distances, difficulty ratings or safety conditions.

## 8. Practical Travel Guide Template

**Objective:** Answer a specific question travellers have before visiting a destination.

### Recommended structure

1. Identify the traveller's question.
2. Give the direct answer.
3. Explain the available options.
4. Provide verified costs or requirements.
5. Mention important limitations.
6. Give a practical recommendation.
7. Invite viewers to save the guide.

### Voiceover example

"Planning to visit [destination]? Here are the important things to know before you go.

First, [location and access information].

For transport, you can consider [verified options].

When planning your budget, remember to account for [relevant expenses].

Before travelling, confirm [fees, operating arrangements or requirements].

Save this guide to help plan your next trip."

## 9. Transport and Journey Template

**Objective:** Explain how to travel between two destinations.

### Video structure

**Opening:**

"Planning to travel from [origin] to [destination]? Let's look at the journey."

**Route:**

"Your journey takes you through [verified route or towns]."

**Transport:**

"Available options include [transport modes]."

**Practical details:**

"Consider [journey duration, fares, booking arrangements and transfers]."

**Advice:**

"Check current schedules and fares before departure."

**Closing:**

"Where should our next journey take us?"

### Visual sequence

1. Starting point.
2. Route map or relevant road/train footage.
3. Journey scenery.
4. Transport experience.
5. Destination arrival.

Never present estimated journey times or fares as confirmed current information.

## 10. Accommodation and Hotel Feature Template

**Objective:** Introduce accommodation options and help viewers make informed choices.

### Production brief

- Property: [Name]
- Location: [Location]
- Accommodation type: [Hotel/lodge/camp/resort]
- Target traveller: [Audience]
- Verified facilities: [Facilities]
- Price information: [Price and date checked]
- Experience status: [Personally visited / Research-based feature / Sponsored visit]

### Voiceover structure

**Opening:**

"Looking for somewhere to stay when visiting [destination]?"

**Introduction:**

"Let's take a closer look at [property], located in [location]."

**Features:**

"The property offers [verified facilities and services]."

**Location:**

"One thing to consider is its location in relation to [attractions, transport or other relevant places]."

**Practical information:**

"Before booking, check room availability, current rates and what is included."

**Closing:**

"Would you consider staying here on your next trip?"

### Editorial rules

- Do not claim a personal stay unless it happened.
- Do not invent room rates or facilities.
- Distinguish an independent review from a promotional feature.
- Disclose sponsorships and affiliate relationships.
- Verify booking details before publication.

## 11. Travel Storytelling Template

**Objective:** Create an emotional connection through a journey or genuine travel experience.

### Story structure

**Opening — Set the scene**

"Some journeys are about more than reaching a destination."

**Beginning — Introduce the journey**

"This is [destination/journey], where [relevant context]."

**Middle — Build the experience**

Show the scenery, people, landscapes and moments that make the journey meaningful.

**Reflection — Add meaning**

"What makes this experience special is [genuine observation or reflection]."

**Ending — Leave viewers with something memorable**

"Sometimes, the best way to understand a place is to experience it."

### Visual direction

- Opening wide shot.
- Movement and journey footage.
- Details of the surroundings.
- People or cultural scenes where appropriate and respectfully filmed.
- Final memorable scenic shot.

First-person accounts must reflect genuine experience. Research-based stories must be presented as such.

## 12. Destination Comparison Template

**Objective:** Help viewers compare two destinations or travel experiences.

### Structure

**Hook:**

"Choosing between [destination A] and [destination B]? Here are some things to consider."

**Comparison:**

- Location and accessibility.
- Main attractions.
- Activities.
- Accommodation choices.
- Travel costs.
- Best time to visit.
- Type of traveller each destination may suit.

**Recommendation:**

"If you prefer [experience], consider [destination]. If you are looking for [different experience], [other destination] may suit you better."

**Closing:**

"Which one would you choose?"

Only compare information that is verified and reasonably comparable.

## 13. YouTube Long-Form Video Template

**Objective:** Create detailed travel videos that build trust and encourage repeat viewing.

### Suggested structure

**1. Opening (0–30 seconds)**

Show the strongest footage and explain what the viewer will discover.

**2. Introduction (30–90 seconds)**

Introduce the destination and explain why the video is useful.

**3. Destination overview**

Cover location, attractions, environment and background.

**4. The journey**

Show transport, route, scenery and arrival where relevant.

**5. Main experience**

Explore activities, wildlife, attractions or accommodation.

**6. Practical travel information**

Cover access, costs, visitor requirements, accommodation and preparation.

**7. Final thoughts**

Summarise the experience or most useful information.

**8. Closing**

Invite viewers to subscribe and suggest the next destination to explore.

Long-form videos should tell a coherent story rather than simply combine unrelated clips.

## 17. Research and Accuracy Checklist

Before writing a script, establish:

- What is the subject?
- Who is the intended audience?
- What does the viewer need to learn?
- What information is confirmed?
- What information requires further checking?
- Is the content based on personal experience or research?
- Are prices and requirements current?
- Are official sources available?
- Are there safety, access or conservation considerations?

Use official tourism authorities, park authorities, transport providers and accommodation sources where appropriate.

When sources disagree, investigate the difference and avoid presenting uncertain information as fact.

## 18. Final Production Checklist

### Before recording

- [ ] Confirm the topic and objective.
- [ ] Select a suitable hook.
- [ ] Verify factual information.
- [ ] Prepare the voiceover.
- [ ] Arrange relevant footage.
- [ ] Identify the CTA.

### During editing

- [ ] Use a strong opening visual.
- [ ] Keep the voiceover natural.
- [ ] Match visuals to the narration.
- [ ] Add readable on-screen text.
- [ ] Use captions where appropriate.
- [ ] Remove unnecessary pauses.
- [ ] Use music that does not overpower narration.
- [ ] Check video quality and sound.

### Before publishing

- [ ] Write a clear title.
- [ ] Prepare the caption or description.
- [ ] Add relevant hashtags.
- [ ] Verify names and locations.
- [ ] Check prices and dates.
- [ ] Disclose sponsorships where applicable.
- [ ] Confirm the video delivers what its hook promises.
- [ ] Choose one relevant CTA.

## 19. Performance Review

After publishing, record:

- Video topic.
- Content pillar.
- Platform.
- Publication date.
- Video duration.
- Hook used.
- Views.
- Average watch time.
- Completion rate, where available.
- Shares.
- Saves.
- Comments.
- Followers or subscribers gained.

Compare videos with similar objectives and formats.

Use the results to improve future hooks, storytelling, editing and topic selection.

## 20. Core Production Principle

Simo Traveller should combine attractive visuals with information and storytelling that make the content worth watching.

**Do not just show people a destination. Give them a reason to discover it, understand it and experience it.**
