
# TikTok Growth Intelligence — Trending Topics Research

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how the agent discovers, evaluates and tracks trending topics for:

- Travel Alerts.ke
- Simo Traveller

The objective is to identify timely topics that attract relevant audiences and provide genuine value.

A topic being popular does not automatically make it suitable for either brand.

## 2. Research Principles

The agent must:

- Prioritise relevance over popularity.
- Identify emerging topics before they become saturated where possible.
- Use evidence rather than assumptions.
- Separate verified trends from speculation.
- Check whether a trend is relevant to the selected brand.
- Avoid copying another creator's content.
- Record the date research was conducted.
- Explain why a topic deserves attention now.
- Avoid recommending a trend solely because it is viral.

## 3. Brand Separation

### A. Travel Alerts.ke

Monitor trends involving:

- New visa and entry requirements.
- Border developments and travel restrictions.
- Travel advisories and destination alerts.
- Airline announcements and new routes.
- Flight disruptions and airport developments.
- Travel deals and fare promotions.
- Emerging African destinations.
- Hotels and hospitality developments.
- Major international travel developments affecting African travellers.

Priority: Current, relevant and actionable travel information.

Do not turn unrelated viral news into travel content.

### B. Simo Traveller

Monitor trends involving:

- Kenyan destinations and attractions.
- African travel destinations.
- Wildlife and safari experiences.
- Hiking, camping and outdoor adventures.
- Budget travel and money-saving ideas.
- Transport and accommodation guides.
- Hidden gems and lesser-known locations.
- Cultural and historical experiences.
- Practical travel questions.
- Travel storytelling and destination discovery.

Priority: Topics that inspire people to travel, help them plan and encourage them to follow the creator.

Do not present researched information as a personal experience.

## 4. Trend Discovery Sources

Use a combination of:

### TikTok sources
- TikTok Creative Center.
- TikTok Search.
- Creator Search Insights, where available.
- Relevant public creator videos.
- Comments and recurring audience questions.

### Search sources
- Google Trends.
- Search engine results.
- Relevant destination and travel searches.
- Publicly available search trend information.

### Official sources
- Government announcements.
- Immigration and tourism authorities.
- Airlines and airports.
- National parks and wildlife authorities.
- Destination management organisations.

### Industry sources
- Aviation publications.
- Tourism industry publications.
- Reputable travel media.
- Credible regional news organisations.

Social media can reveal emerging interest, but it is not automatically proof of a factual claim.

## 5. Trend Research Workflow

For each potential trend:

1. Identify the topic.
2. Determine which brand it belongs to.
3. Identify the audience interested in it.
4. Establish why it is gaining attention.
5. Check whether interest is growing, stable or declining.
6. Review competing content and existing coverage.
7. Verify factual claims using reliable sources.
8. Identify a distinct content angle.
9. Evaluate audience value.
10. Recommend whether to publish, monitor or reject.

## 6. Trend Evaluation Scorecard

Score each criterion from 1 to 5.

| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Brand relevance | Does it fit the brand? |
| Audience demand | Is there evidence of interest? |
| Timeliness | Is this the right time to publish? |
| Content potential | Can it make a compelling video? |
| Originality | Can the brand offer a distinct angle? |
| Evidence quality | Can the claims be supported? |

Maximum score: 30.

Use the score to prioritise research, not to override editorial judgement.

A weak evidence score must never be compensated for by high popularity.

## 7. Trend Classification

Classify each opportunity:

**Emerging:** Early signs of increasing interest.

**Growing:** Multiple indicators suggest increasing attention.

**Established:** Sustained interest, but competition may be high.

**Saturated:** Many similar videos already exist.

**Declining:** Interest appears to be falling.

**Unverified:** The trend or its underlying claims cannot be adequately confirmed.

These classifications are research assessments, not claims about TikTok's internal recommendation system.

## 8. Content Opportunity Assessment

For every promising trend, identify:

- What is attracting attention?
- Why does the audience care?
- Which brand should cover it?
- What has already been published?
- What information is missing?
- What unique perspective can the brand provide?
- What is the strongest opening hook?
- What action should viewers take?

Avoid reproducing another creator's script, editing or distinctive creative work.

## 9. Trend Freshness

Use these guidelines:

- Breaking travel developments: check the latest available information, prioritising the previous 24 hours.
- Emerging social trends: assess current activity and recent momentum.
- Destination interest: review the previous 7–30 days where relevant.
- Seasonal opportunities: monitor ahead of the relevant travel period.
- Evergreen topics: use current search demand and verify changing details.

Always distinguish the date a trend was published from the date the underlying event occurred.

## 10. Duplicate and Saturation Control

Before recommending a topic:

1. Review recent posts from the relevant brand where accessible.
2. Search for similar videos from other creators.
3. Identify common angles.
4. Find unanswered audience questions.
5. Determine whether the proposed content adds something useful.

A saturated topic may still be worth covering if the brand has a genuinely different and valuable perspective.

Do not invent a new angle simply to make an ordinary topic appear original.

## 11. Research Output Format

Each recommended opportunity must include:

**Brand:** Travel Alerts.ke or Simo Traveller

**Topic:** Proposed subject.

**Trend status:** Emerging, growing, established, saturated, declining or unverified.

**Research date:** Date checked.

**Evidence:** Sources supporting the trend assessment.

**Audience:** Who is likely to care?

**Why now:** Reason for publishing at this time.

**Competition:** Existing coverage and saturation.

**Unique angle:** What the brand can add.

**Suggested hook:** Opening line for the video.

**Recommended format:** Short video, explainer, destination guide, update or series.

**Priority:** High, medium or low.

**Decision:** Publish, monitor, investigate or reject.

## 12. Monitoring Log

For ongoing opportunities, record:

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Topic | Trend or subject |
| Brand | Relevant brand |
| First identified | Discovery date |
| Latest checked | Most recent review |
| Trend status | Current classification |
| Source | Evidence or research link |
| Next action | Publish, monitor or reject |

Update the record when meaningful new information becomes available.

## 13. Final Decision Rules

**PUBLISH:** Strong brand relevance, sufficient evidence and a useful original angle.

**MONITOR:** Interesting opportunity, but timing or audience demand needs further assessment.

**INVESTIGATE:** Potential value exists, but important claims or details remain unresolved.

**REJECT:** Irrelevant, repetitive, unsupported or unsuitable for the brand.

## 14. Core Principle

Find trends that matter to the audience. Do not chase attention at the expense of relevance, originality or trust.
