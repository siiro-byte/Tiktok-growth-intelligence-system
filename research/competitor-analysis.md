
# TikTok Growth Intelligence — Competitor Analysis

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how the AI agent studies competing and influential creators to understand content performance, audience behaviour, positioning and growth opportunities.

The analysis supports two separate brands:

- Travel Alerts.ke
- Simo Traveller

The objective is to learn from observable evidence, identify gaps and develop distinctive content strategies.

Competitor analysis must never become content copying.

## 2. Core Principles

The agent must:

- Study relevant creators, not just the largest accounts.
- Prioritise audience overlap and content relevance.
- Analyse individual videos as well as overall accounts.
- Separate observable facts from assumptions.
- Record when the research was conducted.
- Avoid treating views as proof of business success.
- Identify repeatable content principles, not copy creative work.
- Compare creators using appropriate context.
- Respect copyright and intellectual property.
- Never invent competitor statistics or performance data.

## 4. Competitor Selection

Select competitors based on:

1. Similar audience.
2. Similar content category.
3. Geographic relevance.
4. Consistent publishing activity.
5. Useful content formats.
6. Evidence of audience engagement.
7. Distinctive positioning.

Do not select accounts based on follower count alone.

Classify accounts as:

**Direct competitor:** Offers similar content to the same audience.

**Indirect competitor:** Competes for the audience's attention through related content.

**Inspiration account:** Demonstrates useful formats, storytelling or production techniques.

**Authority account:** Official or established source with reliable subject expertise.

## 5. Account Analysis

For each selected account, record:

- Creator or organisation name.
- Platform.
- Public account URL.
- Main content category.
- Geographic focus.
- Target audience.
- Approximate follower count, if visible.
- Posting frequency.
- Main content formats.
- Distinctive positioning.
- Strongest observable content themes.
- Research date.

Do not infer private audience demographics or business revenue.

## 6. Individual Video Analysis

Study relevant videos and record:

- Video topic.
- Publication date.
- Video duration.
- Opening hook.
- Main subject.
- Story structure.
- Editing approach.
- On-screen text.
- Voiceover or presentation style.
- Caption approach.
- Hashtag use.
- Publicly visible views.
- Publicly visible likes and comments, where available.
- Audience questions and recurring reactions.

Analyse why a video may have attracted attention, but label explanations as hypotheses unless supported by stronger evidence.

## 7. Performance Interpretation

Use publicly visible metrics carefully.

### Views
Indicate the number of video plays reported by the platform.

### Likes
Provide a limited indication of audience response.

### Comments
Can reveal questions, objections, interest and audience needs.

### Shares
Can indicate that viewers found content worth distributing, where visible.

### Saves
Can indicate practical value, where visible.

Important limitations:

- Public metrics do not reveal complete account analytics.
- Views do not establish watch time or retention.
- High engagement does not prove follower conversion.
- A single successful video does not establish a repeatable formula.
- Comparisons should consider account size, video age, topic and format.

Never claim to know a competitor's private analytics.

## 8. Hook and Content Structure Analysis

Identify:

- How quickly the video communicates its subject.
- Whether the opening creates curiosity.
- Whether the video establishes a clear benefit.
- How information is organised.
- Whether the pacing maintains interest.
- Whether the ending gives viewers a reason to act.
- Whether the format can be adapted ethically.

Extract principles, not scripts.

## 9. Brand-Specific Competitive Insights

### A. Travel Alerts.ke

Look for opportunities involving:

- Faster identification of significant travel developments.
- Better use of official government and authority sources.
- Clearer explanations of who is affected.
- Practical traveller instructions.
- Underreported African destinations and developments.
- Stronger regional coverage.
- More useful visa, border and airline explainers.

The brand's differentiation should be accurate, current and practical travel intelligence.

Do not imitate sensational headlines or publish unverified alerts for attention.

### B. Simo Traveller

Look for opportunities involving:

- Destinations receiving insufficient attention.
- More useful travel planning information.
- Better storytelling through scenery and voiceover.
- Budget-conscious travel ideas.
- Practical transport and accommodation guides.
- Wildlife and outdoor experiences.
- Stronger recurring content series.
- Audience questions that existing creators overlook.

The brand's differentiation should combine discovery, practical value and trustworthy storytelling.

Do not claim personal experience where none exists.

## 10. Content Gap Identification

For every competitor category, investigate:

1. Which questions are repeatedly unanswered?
2. Which destinations receive little useful coverage?
3. Which travel developments lack practical explanations?
4. Which topics are covered superficially?
5. Which audiences are underserved?
6. Which formats could explain the subject better?
7. Which opportunities fit the brand's resources?

A content gap must represent genuine audience value, not merely an opportunity to publish something different.

## 11. Competitor Comparison Scorecard

Score relevant accounts from 1 to 5.

| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Audience relevance | Similarity to target audience |
| Content consistency | Regularity and coherence |
| Content usefulness | Practical value |
| Originality | Distinctive perspective |
| Storytelling | Clarity and engagement |
| Trustworthiness | Quality of sourcing and claims |

Use the score to organise research, not to declare an absolute winner.

## 12. Research Output Format

For each competitor analysis, provide:

**Brand:** Travel Alerts.ke or Simo Traveller

**Competitor:** Account name.

**Platform:** TikTok or another relevant platform.

**Account link:** Public URL.

**Research date:** Date checked.

**Positioning:** What the account is known for.

**Audience overlap:** Why its audience is relevant.

**Content strengths:** Observable strengths.

**Video examples:** Relevant public videos and links.

**Performance observations:** Available public metrics.

**Hook patterns:** Common opening approaches.

**Content gaps:** Opportunities not adequately addressed.

**Lessons:** Principles worth adapting.

**Risks:** What should not be copied or repeated.

**Recommended action:** Specific improvement for our brand.

## 13. Monitoring and Review

Review priority competitors periodically.

Track:

- New content formats.
- Changes in publishing frequency.
- Emerging subject areas.
- Repeated high-performing themes.
- Audience questions.
- Changes in positioning.
- New opportunities for differentiation.

Do not assume a competitor's strategy remains effective indefinitely.

Review findings against our own account performance.

## 14. Ethical and Copyright Standards

The agent must never:

- Copy scripts or captions.
- Reproduce another creator's distinctive creative work.
- Re-upload videos without permission.
- Misrepresent competitor performance.
- Use private information.
- Present assumptions as verified facts.
- Encourage harassment or negative targeting of creators.

Public content may be analysed for learning and competitive research.

## 15. Final Decision

Every competitor research exercise should produce practical recommendations.

**ADAPT:** A general principle can improve our content.

**DIFFERENTIATE:** A content gap creates an opportunity for original work.

**MONITOR:** A development deserves further observation.

**IGNORE:** The competitor's approach is irrelevant or unsuitable.

## 16. Core Principle

Study the competition to understand the audience better, improve our work and build a distinctive brand.

Learn from the market. Do not become a copy of it.
