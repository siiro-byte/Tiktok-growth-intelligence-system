**Projects covered:** Travel Alerts.ke and Simo Traveller. Analyse the two brands separately.

**Purpose:** Provide a repeatable source base for TikTok and YouTube growth research, content experiments, audience discovery and competitor observation.

## Travel Alerts.ke content research sources

Use the official source hierarchy in the [Travel Alerts.ke source registry](https://github.com/siiro-byte/travel-alerts-ke/blob/main/SOURCES.md) and the editorial rules in the master guide. A trend is not a reason to publish an unverified travel claim.

Monitor:
- Travel-alert topics with clear and current traveller impact.
- Destination discovery and unusual places with verified visitor information.
- Search interest around routes, borders, airports, destinations and travel rules.
- Public comments and questions as audience-intent signals, not factual proof.
- Competitor public posts for format and topic comparison, not as authoritative sources.

## Audience and community signals

- TikTok and YouTube comments on relevant public videos.
- Reddit: https://www.reddit.com/ — first-hand traveller discussions as leads only.
- Destination forums and public traveller reviews.
- Search suggestions and related queries from platform-native search.

Record the date, country, destination, route and context. Distinguish first-hand reports from hearsay, and verify material claims independently before using them as facts.

## Competitor observation

For each brand, select relevant accounts separately and record:
- Account and platform URL.
- Content pillar and topic.
- Opening hook and first-frame visual.
- Duration and presentation format.
- Caption, keywords and call to action.
- Public views, likes, comments and shares at the observation time.
- Date observed and age of the post.
- What may be transferable and what is brand-specific.

Public engagement is not proof of reach, watch time, conversion or sales. Do not infer competitor ad spend or private analytics.

## Required research record

| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| Brand | Travel Alerts.ke or Simo Traveller |
| Source / account | Exact name and URL |
| Source class | First-party analytics, official source, public competitor, media or community lead |
| Topic / experiment | What is being researched |
| Date observed | Date and timezone |
| Metric and definition | Exact platform metric |
| Evidence | What the source actually shows |
| Interpretation | Clearly separated from evidence |
| Confidence | Confirmed, partly confirmed, unverified or unavailable |
| Next test | Practical experiment or follow-up |

## Cost and account safeguards

- Default research spend is KES 0.
- Use free/public sources and existing included account access first.
- No paid analytics, credits, subscriptions, upgrades, ads or API calls without explicit fresh approval.
- Before a paid operation, disclose provider, operation, data requested, estimated cost and maximum if known, currency and KES equivalent, free alternatives; then wait for approval.
- Never bypass credit limits, mix the two brands' analytics or change account connections without approval.

**Research principle:** Platform signals suggest what to test. First-party analytics determine what actually worked. Brand trust and factual accuracy take priority over trend chasing.
